# Kandela Kulture: Manifesto

*Final. This replaces Parts One and Two. The mission has not changed. It has gained depth.*

## The spark

Candela is the measure of light: how brightly a flame burns, and in which direction. We took the word, bent it with a K, and gave it a heartbeat.

Kandela is about the small, stubborn flame: the one you carry into a dark room, the one you shield with your hands, the one that lights another and loses nothing in the giving.

## What we noticed

First meetings are easy to have and hard to keep. People share a dance floor, an office, a congregation or a football pitch for years and know each other only one way. They talk, but never do anything together. Or they work side by side for a decade and never sit down and really talk. The spark is there. It has nowhere to go.

A relationship that lives in one room is fragile. When the job, the team or the street changes, it ends.

Dance showed us this most clearly. Dance communities build deep trust, week after week, and almost none of it ever leaves the floor.

## What we do

**People get to know each other in two ways: face to face, and side by side.** Most groups only get one. So we give them the other.

If your people meet face to face, we organise something side by side. If they work or dance side by side, we make time face to face. Then something new happens. What you do together becomes a third context: a story, a running joke, a shared effort that belongs to your group and to no one else.

We begin with dance, because dance communities already share deep trust and the missing context is the lowest-hanging fruit. But Kandela is not about dance. It is for any group that would like to know each other another way: a work team, a campus club, a congregation, a street. For a team it can become a way of life, a rhythm of small, repeated moments in place of a single away-day.

**Rest that still moves you.** Everything we offer is tied to something you are already trying to get done. It is a break from routine that points the same way as your goals, never a detour or a prize for finishing the real work. So we ask one question before we design anything: what are you already trying to get done this year?

## How we do it

We build fun infrastructure. Every session moves through four steps.

- **Spark.** We open with play, a task that needs cooperation and little talk. Play lowers defences, and people connect and learn more when their guard is down.
- **Pivot.** We move people from face to face to side by side, or the other way round, whichever they were missing.
- **Layer.** We add a second and third context: a skill, a service, a making task, a walk. Each shows another side of the people in the room.
- **Pass.** Every session ends with a toolkit, so the group can run it without us.

Three things hold it together:

- **Hosts are architects, not preachers.** A host sets the frame, gives the prompt and steps back. Nobody is lectured. People drive.
- **Interaction is a must.** We don't put on a show for you. We make a room you can act in.
- **We begin general, then curate.** The same bones work for a dance community, a company, a school and an NGO. Then we shape them to what that group needs.

## What stays

We leave something behind. A skill: how to listen when things are tense, how to hold a circle for a friend who is struggling, how to cook for forty, how to decide something together. Where a skill can save a life or carries real risk, we teach it only alongside accredited providers, because we are the container and the convenor, not the certifier.

We leave a toolkit, a small set of things your group can use to run the session itself. And we leave a ritual.

## Pass the flame

Every local community is a **Hearth**, lit from another by passing a flame from hand to hand. Members of any Hearth are welcome at all of them.

## What we believe

**Light is meant to be passed on.** A flame loses nothing by lighting another.

**Brightness needs direction.** Candela measures light in a direction. We don't glow for the sake of glowing. We point toward connection.

**Spotlights isolate. Fires gather.** We are not building stars. We are building a circle with room for everyone.

**The dark is not the enemy.** It is where we start. We don't run from it. We bring something into it.

**Fun and substance belong together.** Fun lowers defences. Substance makes it last.

**Service comes first.** Lamplighters give time to the places we sit in, and every Hearth leaves something behind.

**Credit is generosity.** Everyone who contributes, in any discipline, is named and fairly paid.

## What we promise

1. **Fun that leaves something behind:** a skill, a ritual or a toolkit.
2. **Members are never the product.** Contact details are never sold or shared.
3. **Consent first,** for photos, for data and for taking part.
4. **Real skills from the right people.** Anything risky is taught only by accredited providers.
5. **Everyone credited and fairly paid.**
6. **We say only what we know.** We report what we learn, including what doesn't work. Where we are still testing an idea, such as whether a rhythm beats a retreat, we say so.
7. **We give the method away carefully.** It is written down so it can outgrow us.

## How it grows

Hearths grow from Hearths. Keepers host and look after the room. Lamplighters volunteer, and you don't need to dance, you need to turn up. Flames, our poets, designers, musicians, filmmakers and cooks, plug in their craft. Partners, from studios and companies to schools, NGOs and accredited providers, help us reach further. Everyone starts as a spark and can become the one who lights the next.

## What we measure

We count connection, not attendance: how many different contexts each pair of people shares, what actually passes between them, whether groups keep going without us, and whether it would have happened without us. We publish the results, including the disappointing ones.

## The invitation

If you dance, you already hold more trust than you realise.

If you run a team, a studio, a school or a space, you may have a room full of people who have only ever met one way.

If you want to help, you don't need to be good at anything. You need to turn up.

We create a context for continuity, so that first impressions can grow into lasting bonds. We give people a different way to meet that builds on where they began and lets more of each other come to light. After the sun sets, we keep the light on by letting you pass your flame to others.

**We are Kandela Kulture.**

**Light the Way. Join the Kulture.**

*Kuna nuru gizani: there is light in the darkness.*
