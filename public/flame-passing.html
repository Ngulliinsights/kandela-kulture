Before the ceremony

A new Hearth doesn’t appear overnight. It is grounded in three living commitments:

Prerequisite 01

A qualified Keeper

Someone who has joined at least 5 gatherings as a participant, feels the room rhythm intuitively, and knows how to host without dominating.

5+ gatherings · Vetted by Hearth
Prerequisite 02

A local community

An existing group ready for deeper connection: a dance community, a team, a creative studio, a campus collective, or a neighborhood circle.

Living soil · Eager to gather
Prerequisite 03

A 6-month commitment

A pact to hold regular gatherings for at least 6 months, supported by weekly check-ins and mentorship from the originating Hearth.

Bi-weekly cadence · Tended fire

The originating Hearth reviews applications through dialogue, not forms. When values align and readiness is confirmed, we schedule the Lighting ceremony.

The ceremony

A real gathering, not an abstract ritual. The room is open, participants arrive, and within the ordinary rhythm of a Kandela session, the transfer occurs.

The flame passes hand to hand

The originating Keeper travels with the new Keeper and a crew of Lamplighters. Near the close of the gathering, a lit candle passes hand to hand through the circle. The new Keeper lights their own hearth candle from it, taking on the responsibility to tend it.

“Light does not diminish by lighting another. It multiplies.”

Act 01 Arrival & Spark

Originating Keeper facilitates · New Keeper attends
  1. 01

    Arrival

    The delegation arrives and the community gathers. The space is arranged for an authentic Kandela session with warm low lighting.

  2. 02

    Spark

    The originating Keeper opens with a non-verbal Spark game. The new Keeper participates as an equal player, not as host.

Act 02 Pivot & Layer

Felt pacing · Shared creation
  1. 03

    Pivot

    The session transitions between face-to-face dialogue and side-by-side action. The new Keeper feels the exact moment the room’s energy softens.

  2. 04

    Layer

    A new craft or collaborative practice is introduced. The travelling team observes how the local group adopts and reshapes it.

Act 03 Commitment & Toolkit

Communal covenant · Passing the container
  1. 05

    Commitment

    The new Keeper voices their pledge to protect the container and safety guidelines. The circle responds with how they will turn up.

  2. 06

    Toolkit

    The session concludes with the physical handover: the method deck, the registry, and the candle. The new Keeper now holds the flame.

After the ceremony

Lighting the candle takes an hour. Rooting the Hearth takes 90 days of deliberate companionship:

Month 01

Weekly check-ins

Weekly debriefs between Keepers: analyzing room dynamics, pacing adjustments, and attendee feedback after every session.

Month 02

Lamplighter support

Experienced Lamplighters travel or join to help with greeting, room setup, and holding the perimeter as attendance grows.

Month 03

Open method feedback

What the new Hearth discovers on its home soil feeds back into the global methodology. Learnings belong to all Hearths.

Day 90 Rooting: At the end of three months, the Hearth operates independently. The flame has taken root.

The Passport & The Network

Carry your thread between cities

With two Hearths, the Passport comes alive. A participant who joins gatherings in Nairobi can travel to Mombasa, Kigali, or London, and walk into a warm room with an identical trust container and familiar faces.
