How a Hearth is lit

Pass the flame

Light carries. One Hearth lights the next, and the pattern continues.

Start a HearthBecome a Keeper

Before the ceremony

A new Hearth doesn't appear overnight. It needs:

A qualified Keeper

who has joined at least 5 gatherings, knows the method and can host.

A local community

that wants to try this: a dance group, a team, a campus club, a street.

A commitment

to run gatherings for at least 6 months, with support from the originating Hearth.

The originating Hearth reviews the application and, if it fits, schedules the ceremony.

The ceremony

The flame passes

A real gathering, not a symbolic ritual. The originating Keeper travels with the new Keeper and Lamplighters. Near the end, a lit candle passes hand to hand. The new Keeper lights their own from it.

Arrival

The delegation arrives and the community gathers. The room is set for a normal Kandela session.

Spark

The originating Keeper opens with a Spark task. The new Keeper plays as a participant, not as host.

Pivot

The session moves through its structure. The new Keeper observes and starts to feel the rhythm.

Layer

A skill or task is shared. The delegation watches how the new group takes it in.

Commitment

The new Keeper says what they will do and how they will hold the space. The community answers with how it will show up.

Toolkit

The session closes with the toolkit. The new Keeper now holds the flame, the method and the tools.

After the ceremony

3 months of support

Weekly Keeper check-ins: what worked, what to adjust. Lamplighters can travel to help with the first gatherings. What the new Hearth learns feeds back into the method.

After 3 months the new Hearth runs on its own. The flame has taken root.

The Passport

With two Hearths, the Passport becomes real. Members of any Hearth are welcome at all of them. Message the Keeper before you travel, and you'll walk into a room of strangers who already know your flame.

Where we are

The first ceremony hasn’t happened yet. When Nairobi is stable, with self-sustaining gatherings, a trained Keeper and a Lamplighter team, we’ll invite applications for the second Hearth and document the ceremony here, with photos and what actually happened.

This is what we intend, and it may change as we learn. We say only what we know.
