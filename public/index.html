A community for dancers

Same people. Different room.

You know each other from the dance floor. Now share a supper, a walk or a day of volunteering. Friendships deepen, networks strengthen, and the rest comes naturally.

Save your place First time? Start here

What happens at a gathering

  1. You come.

    A few people you may already know, and a host who looks after the room.

  2. You do something side by side.

    Cook, walk, build, help out. The task does the talking.

  3. You leave knowing someone better.

    Easy company, new things in common, and no pressure to be anywhere you don’t want to be.
