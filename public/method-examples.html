Method in practice

What a session actually looks like

Four intentional moves that turn an ordinary gathering into an enduring connection container.

Try the methodSee upcoming gatherings

Choose one element from each move. Your facilitation blueprint compiles below in real time.

Move 01 · Play

The Spark

Play first without titles or preambles. High physical cooperation and little speech drops social defenses immediately.

Pick 1 exercise · 10–15 min
Choose a Spark task
Move 02 · Shift

The Pivot

Switch how bodies meet in space — from face-to-face dialogue to side-by-side action, or whichever orientation they were lacking.

Pick 1 orientation · 45 min
Choose a Pivot
Move 03 · Craft

The Layer

Add a shared pursuit. Each layer reveals another facet of the room and creates a tangible anchor you can point back to.

Multi-select · 40 min each
Choose Layers
Move 04 · Handover

The Pass

Every session concludes with a practical toolkit, so the group can run it without facilitators. We are the container, not the centre.

Handover & Departure · 15 min
