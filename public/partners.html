How we work

  • Members come first, and their details are never sold or shared.
  • We measure honestly and report what we find.
  • Good work that grows between people is welcome. We let it happen.

What we never do

  • Sell access to members.
  • Let anyone pitch at a gathering.
  • Overstate results.
